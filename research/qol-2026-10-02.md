# Quality-of-life proposals, 2026-10-02

**Status: built, 2026-10-02.** Every chosen item is in, one commit each (two
pairs share one); see "Built" below. Not yet pushed. What is left is checking
it by hand: there is no browser-driven suite.

## Decided, 2026-10-02

**Build:** everything below except #22 and #29: all eight in tier 1, the
remaining tier-2 items, and #34 in tier 3.

**Declined:**

- **#22, copying a picture to the system clipboard.** Not wanted.
- **#29, an "Always open links" answer.** "Leave mbrd?" keeps asking on every
  external link, as `ui/leaving.ts` intends.

**Settled:**

- **#12:** a held nudge folds into one undo step per run. A pause of about a
  second, another edit or a change of selection ends the run.
- **#2:** note colour stays single-item, as proposed.

## Built, 2026-10-02

Each commit's message names its item and says what it does. Where the build
differs from the proposal below:

- **#28:** a history entry stands guard, and CloseWatcher is not used. A
  CloseWatcher also answers the Escape key, and an Escape with nothing open would
  then be passed on to `history.back()` and leave the app. The guard is re-armed
  on the next press, because Chrome's Back skips an entry pushed without user
  activation. Only where a Back button is the way out: a coarse pointer, or the
  installed app.
- **#23:** "Which?" names the failed files in a second toast, six names and then
  a count. "Show them" is offered only when every new card is outside the view.
- **#21:** the mark is an injected check (`setMissingCheck()` in
  `canvas/item-dom.ts`). Healing takes the hashes that are missing *before* an
  import reads anything, since reading a file registers it. On a card whose file
  is missing, the "Find the file…" row is #34's Replace file… under another name.
- **#9:** cut takes the `travelling()` set too. A paste that brought the notes
  back while the cut left them on the board would give the notes twice.
- **#20:** Undo runs only while its change is the top of the stack. Pressed
  later, it says so instead of undoing something else (`undoAction()`,
  `history.ts`).
- **#30:** the shell report gained an `owned` flag. The desktop and Android
  shells ignore it.

Not checked by hand yet: anything on a phone (#28, #25's lock screen, #32 with
a keyboard), media keys, the offline wait (#27), and how the missing-file mark
looks at each whimsy tier.

Three read-only scouts each covered one surface of the app: the board itself,
getting content in and out, and the menus, settings and platforms around the
board. They made 54 proposals. One verifier then checked each against the code.
It asked whether the feature already exists, whether the cited lines say what
the scout claimed, whether it breaks a rule in `CLAUDE.md` or a decision in
`research/docs/` or the todo, and whether it repeats another proposal. 34
survived. They are below in three tiers. Line numbers are from commit `d7a8d05`.

None of them changes the `.mbrd` schema, the generated catalogues or the
service-worker cache.

## Tier 1: do first

High value, small or medium effort, low risk.

**1. Copy on one board, paste on another (M).** The item menu's comment
promises that paste lands "on any board" (`ui/menu.ts:669`). But `loadBoard()`
clears the clipboard on every load (`state.ts:2122`), because `clearAssets()`
empties the hash-to-Blob registry and the copied drafts hold only hashes
(`clipboard.ts:52-70`). So Copy, then Your boards, then Ctrl+V does nothing.
Today the only way to move cards between boards is a whole `.mbrd` export and a
merge.

- *Change:* On copy or cut, hold `{blob, meta}` for every hash the copied items
  name. Stop clearing on load. On paste, `putAsset()` each missing hash before
  `addItems()`.
- *Where it goes:* `clipboard.ts` is base layer, so the byte carry has to be
  injected from `main.ts`, as `setAssetNameLookup()` is, or kept in the copy and
  paste wrappers in `commands.ts`. Collect the hashes with the existing
  `itemHashes()` walk, not a new list.
- *Watch:* the `tests/layers.test.js` DEBT map has to stay empty. The held Blobs
  stay in memory until the next copy. `emptyTrash()` already protects clipped
  bytes (`trash.ts:437`), and that has to keep working across a switch.

**2. Change a note's paper colour after it is made (S).** A note's tint is set
only when it is created (`import/drop.ts:1146`). Stickers and fences have
`setStickerTint`/`setFenceTint` (`state.ts:1071`, `:1081`), but notes have no
equivalent, and the renderer would already redraw a changed tint
(`canvas/item-dom.ts:652`).

- *Change:* Add a Colour row to a note's menu, offering the four sheets, built
  like `fenceTintEntries()`.
- *Watch:* keep it single-item. The argument in `commands/item-meta.ts:107-113`
  for single-item sticker tint applies here too.

**3. Escape cancels a drag, resize or marquee in progress (S).** The Escape
case (`canvas/input.ts:3286-3308`) never looks at `gesture`. Pressing Escape
mid-drag clears the selection under the hand, and the move still commits.
`abortGesture()` (`input.ts:3024`) already restores `gesture.before` and records
nothing.

- *Change:* One guard at the top of the case: if a gesture is live, abort it.
- *Watch:* the `pointerup` that follows has to find the gesture idle. A press
  that has not yet become a move has to count as cancelled too.

**4. `?` opens the shortcuts legend, and the legend is corrected (S).** `KEYS`
(`ui/settings-schema.ts:256-299`) sits in a closed fold at the bottom of the
System tab, and nothing leads to it. It is also out of date.

- *What is wrong in it:* It says "dbl-click: zoom to an item", which
  `input.ts:3095` records was removed on purpose. The menu still prints that
  accel on "Zoom to it" (`menu.ts:678`). It leaves out Shift-click to add, Shift
  to keep Join armed, double-click a line to label it, and Delete on a marked
  line.
- *Change:* A `shortcuts` cmd, a small exported `reveal(tab, fold)` in
  `ui/panel.ts`, a `?` case in the keydown switch (match on `e.key`), and a Help
  row in `desktop/menu.ts`. It is desktop-only, since on a phone the legend is
  scrolled past on purpose.

**5. Put the next step on toasts that ask for one (S).** `ToastAction` exists
(`notify.ts:19`) but only Update and tour-stop Undo use it. These toasts give an
instruction and no way to carry it out, then vanish after 2.6 s:

- "reload to update" (`main.ts:829`). It needs a Reload button (`cmds.restart`).
- "export a file to keep a durable copy" (`storage/storage.ts:224`). It needs an
  Export button.
- "This browser will not store the board" (`storage/session.ts:300`). It needs
  an Export button.
- "Signed out of Drive" (`main.ts:688`). It needs a Connect button.
- "Nothing is on the tour yet - add cards from their menu" (`ui/tour.ts:105`).
  That menu row no longer exists (`item-meta.ts:242`). The toast needs new text
  and a Create tour button.

**6. Never drop a web board off the shelf silently (S).**
`shelveCurrent()` calls `trimLibrary()` (`storage.ts:1005`). That keeps
`SHELF_MAX = 24` and deletes the oldest blob (`library.ts:117-136`). On the web
that blob is the only copy, and the count it returns is thrown away, so opening
a 25th board erases one.

- *Change:* Before that happens, ask once and name the board, with Export it
  first / Delete it / Cancel. At the least, show a toast naming the board that
  went.
- *Watch:* ask before `stashCurrent()` takes the writer down.
  `tests/library.test.js:125` needs a companion test for the notice.

**7. Keep showing that the browser stopped saving (S).** When IndexedDB refuses
a write, `session.ts:295-301` latches `cacheOk = false` and shows one toast.
Nothing saves after that, and nothing says so. The stated reason for having no
`beforeunload` guard (`session.ts:518`) stops being true in exactly this state.

- *Change:* A standing "Not saved: Export" mark in the `#saved` slot, and a
  `beforeunload` guard armed only while the latch holds.
- *Watch:* trigger this on the failure path's own event, never on `cacheOk`
  alone. `clearAllData()` and `suspendCache()` also set it, for /patch, the
  bench and not-found.

**8. Save or share a card's own file (S).** Every dropped file is kept byte for
byte, but nothing gives it back. `saveBlob()` has one caller, the board export
(`commands/file.ts:51`), so getting a PDF back means unzipping a `.mbrd`, and on
a phone it can't be done at all.

- *Change:* A "Save the file" row in the item menu and the viewer menu. It
  writes `meta.source` or `asset.hash` under `origName`, sanitised as
  `boardArtefactName()` does. Where `navigator.canShare({files})` answers yes,
  it is "Share the file" instead.

## Tier 2: worth it

**9. Duplicate and copy bring their riders and joins (M).** A drag carries
stuck notes and fence contents through `travelling()` (`layout.ts:853`).
Duplicate, copy and cut use `itemsIn(ids)` (`state.ts:434`), and `cloneItem()`
keeps no connection. So Ctrl+D on a photo with three notes gives a bare photo,
and a duplicated fence is empty.

- *Change:* Widen all three through `travelling()`, remap the ids, and copy the
  connections that run between copied cards, in the same commit.
- *Watch:* `meta.stuckTo` has to be remapped, or a copied note reloads stuck to
  the original.

**10. Step through items in the viewer, and Enter opens the selection (M).**
The viewer has no next or previous. Change: Left/Right, two quiet edge buttons,
and a swipe when the picture isn't zoomed, in Feed order or reading order. Never
step while a document is being edited. Each step runs the same teardown as
closing (`ui/viewer.ts`). Add an Enter case to the keydown switch.

**11. Find selects every match, remembers the query, and answers Ctrl+F (S).**
`ui/search.ts` selects one hit, caps the list at 12 with no sign that there are
more, and starts empty every time. Change: Shift+Enter or a "Select all N" row
selects every match. The field pre-fills the last query. Ctrl+F on the board
opens Find in the browser as it already does on the desktop
(`desktop/menu.ts:83`), and the two legend rows become one.

**12. A held arrow-key nudge is one undo step (S).** Each press commits
(`input.ts:3397`). Two seconds held is dozens of entries, and that against
`HISTORY_LIMIT = 200`. Change: fold the run with `commitFolded()`
(`history.ts:105`). Check this with the maintainer, since `history.ts:173`
argues for fine undo granularity.

**13. Shift-drag keeps a move to one axis (S).** `track()` is given `shiftKey`,
but the move branch ignores it (`input.ts:2477-2545`). Snapping and the sticky
override still have to run after the constraint.

**14. A Select fold on the card menu (S).** Its three rows are All
`<kind>`s, What is in this fence (`fenceFollowers()`), and What it is joined to
(`connectedTo()`). Today `selectFiltered` is the only set-select.

**15. Remove the connections of the selected cards in one step (S).** Today it
is one line at a time, or "Remove all" in the Debug fold. Change: add
`removeConnectionsOf(ids)` beside `clearConnections`, with one commit.

**16. Same width / Same height in Align & distribute (M).** Grips work on a
single card only (`input.ts:203`). Change: a pure `matchSizeTargets()` in
`geometry.ts`, with `registerOp`, so the step can be edited on the timeline,
which the todo's first item asks for.

**17. Shift+F zooms to the selection, and 1 sets 100% in place (S).** Today F
always fits the whole board, and 100% is only a click on the readout. Disable 1
under a zoom lock.

**18. Single keys for tools and rows that have none (S).** N for a note, J for
Join, Ctrl+G to fence the selection, L to anchor, and ] / [ for front and back.
Each one also goes in its menu accel, its toolbar title and `KEYS`.

**19. Say what Undo and Redo just did (S).** Today an off-screen undo is
silent. Change: a brief "Undid Nudge" beside `#history-ctl`, on the `#saved`
timer, announced politely. It is not a toast, so holding Ctrl+Z doesn't flood
the stack.

**20. Undo on the receipts of large changes (S).** Covers "Cut N to the bin",
"Joined N pairs", "Removed N connections", and a new receipt for Rearrange. The
Undo runs only if `lastCommand()` is still that step.

**21. Mark cards whose file is missing, and heal them (M).** After "N items
came back without their data" (`session.ts:407`), every save fails and the
broken cards look blank. Change: derive a "missing" state with the
`itemHashes()` walk. Give the toasts a "Show them" action and the card a "Find
the file…" row. A file that arrives with a matching hash heals the card.

**22. Copy a picture to the system clipboard (S). Declined.** Ctrl+C on a photo puts only
text on the system clipboard, and nothing calls `ClipboardItem`. Change: a "Copy
picture" row on a card and in the viewer, and a Copy button in the Save image
box. It writes PNG only, passing a `Promise<Blob>` for Safari.

**23. The import summary names failures and can show the cards (S).** The
failed names reach only the console (`drop.ts:533`, `:631`), and the bus
`imported` event has no listener. Change: the toast gets one action. "Which?"
lists the failures as text nodes. With no failures, "Show them" frames new cards
that landed off screen.

**24. Export only the selection, or one fence (M).** `planRender()` always
takes every drawable item (`ui/snapshot.ts:208`, `:1441`). Put the `only`
filter in `planRender` so it survives the DOM-export rewrite
(`export-from-dom-2026-09-21.md`).

**25. Media keys and lock-screen controls for the board's music (S).**
`navigator.mediaSession` appears nowhere in the code. Publish the now-playing
title and art from `ui/nowplaying.ts`, with play/pause/next/previous/seek
handlers.

**26. A YouTube link keeps its timestamp, and music.youtube.com plays (S).**
`embedFor()` drops every parameter (`canvas/embed.ts:110`). Change: carry `t`
over as a bounded `?start=`. No CSP change.

**27. Cloud sync waits out an offline spell (S).** An offline push latches "Cloud
sync stopped" in red (`main.ts:696`), and nothing listens for `online`. Change:
when `navigator.onLine === false`, show a quiet note and retry on `online`. The
conflict path still latches.

**28. The phone's Back closes the top layer on the web and in the PWA (M).**
The web `onBack` is a no-op (`platform/platform.ts:377`). Change: use
CloseWatcher where it exists, and a one-entry `pushState` sentinel elsewhere.
Keep it off on the desktop and in Capacitor.

**29. "Leave mbrd?" can be answered once per device (S). Declined: it keeps
asking.** Every external link
click asks (`ui/leaving.ts`). Change: an "Always open links" answer, and a check
in This browser to bring the question back. It is on by default and stored per
device only. `leaving.ts` frames the guard as deliberate, so this is the
maintainer's call.

**30. Name the board in the browser tab (S).** On the web, `setDocument` is a
no-op, so every tab carries the marketing title. Change: "`<title>` - mbrd" once
the board has a name or content. Keep the static title on an empty default
board, for SEO.

**31. The Menu key and Shift+F10 open the item menu (S).** Today a keyboard
user can select a card through Find but cannot reach its actions. Change: anchor
the menu at the card's screen box, through `cmds.contextMenu`.

**32. Focus follows the sidebar (S).** `ui/sidebar.ts` never moves focus.
Change: a keyboard open focuses the current tab, and a close returns focus to
`#menu-btn`, using the rule `closeLibrary()` already uses.

**33. Toasts with a button wait while pointed at or focused (S).** Today they
retire on a bare timer (`ui/overlays.ts:40`). This matters more once items 5, 20
and 23 add buttons.

## Tier 3: nice to have

**34. Replace a card's file in place (M).** A "Replace file…" row runs
`prepareFile()` and then `retypeItem()`/`carryRelations()` (`state.ts:628-668`),
keeping the card's place, width, tags and joins. Tags, lock and fit have to be
merged on purpose, because `retypeItem` builds meta from the new item.

## Dropped by the verifier

- **Merged into a kept item:** a second `?` legend, a second cross-board paste,
  the empty-tour message, Ctrl+F, and the "Show them" import action.
- **Double-click zooms to a card with nothing to open:** this goes against the
  decision recorded at `input.ts:3095`.
- **Card-look toggles on a multi-selection:** `item-meta.ts` says widening a
  `can*` is a product decision.
- **A tap on a binned card restores it:** this goes against the drawer's
  stated design (`drawer.ts:2`).
- **Desktop boards in the OS recent list:** a path never comes from the page.
- **Paste through the menu:** `clipboard.read` permissions are uncertain, and
  even more so in the Capacitor WebView.
- **Cut for value or effort:** Alt-drag to copy, camera jump-back, alignment
  guides while dragging, pasting a link list, a shelf filter, duplicating a board
  from the shelf, an undo for deleting a shelf board, settings in Ctrl+K, ⌘ on
  Apple machines, an Install button, and a duplicate-import notice.
