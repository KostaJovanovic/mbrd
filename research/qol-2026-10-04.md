# Quality-of-life plan, 2026-10-04

**Status: open.** Nothing built yet. The work that prompted it is already in:
Nudge board (`450b9c9`), Rearrange fence keeping the fence's size (`450b9c9`),
fence walls pushing cards on a resize (`450b9c9`), and a click on a fence's body
picking the fence (`ccb9b72`).

Three read-only scans of the code found 38 possible changes. The 31 worth
offering were put to the maintainer, who picked 23 on 2026-10-04 (Alt+drag is
two items below, so 24 entries). They are grouped into batches that touch the
same files, in the order to build them. Each item is one commit. Paths are under
`web/assets/js/`.

**Not chosen:** Add a note here on the board's menu, the fence drop-target
highlight, a Duplicate menu row, colouring several cards at once, Shift+R and
Shift+N, a Rename row on the title card, and resizing a group with one set of
handles.

## Batch 1: things that misbehave today

**1. Align, Distribute and Same size carry what rides the cards.**
`alignIds`, `distributeIds` and `matchSizeIds` (`commands.ts:135-165`) write
only their own ids, so a note stuck to an aligned card stays where it was, while
its record still says it is stuck. The comment at `commands.ts:348` claims the
opposite. After `applyGeom(targets)`, walk the riders of the moved ids the way
`rearrange()` and `nudgeBoard()` do: snapshot them in `before`, then
`stuckPlacement(note, hostBefore, hostNow)` in passes, so a note on a note
waits for its host. Same size moves a note by its fraction of the card, which
is the rule a resize already follows. The replay runners
(`registerLayoutOps`) need it too, so the walk goes in a helper that all three
call.
*Keeping cards in their fence:* a card whose fence is not itself being aligned,
and whose new centre would fall outside that fence, grows the fence to keep it,
using the grow step from `nudgeBoard()`. A locked fence does not grow, and the
card leaves it as it does today.

**2. Dropped and pasted files land clear of existing cards.**
`import/drop.ts:254` passes `avoidOverlap` only for a folder, and the paste at
`:283` passes nothing, so new cards cover old ones. On the canvas, always give
`arrange()` the board's cards as obstacles, near the drop point, as `fromFolder`
already does (`:752-755`). Links cascading from the drop point (`:1523-1537`)
take the same obstacles. The Feed packs its own column, so it is unaffected.

**3. The Feed's multi-select menu stops offering rows that refuse.**
Align & distribute and Fence these N are shown in the Feed and answer "…is a
canvas thing". Add `mobile ||` to their `hidden` flags (`ui/menu.ts:785-790`),
as Reset size and Zoom already have it. The toasts stay for the keyboard and the
console.

**4. One darkroom Apply is one undo step.**
`ui/darkroom.ts:533-557` commits crop, turn, grade, flip and matte separately,
and `refitCard` adds a geometry write, so one edit takes up to six presses of
Ctrl+Z. Fold them into one history entry, "Edit picture". Check first how
`commit()` folds entries (the `fold` option on `commitGeom` is one way), and
take the darkroom redesign below into account.

**5. Dragging a fence brings its locked cards along.**
`canvas/input.ts:1761-1765` filters locked cards out of `travelling()`, so they
stay put, and on release they are measured out of the fence without a word. The
maintainer chose to carry them: a lock stops the card itself from being grabbed,
not the fence it sits in from being moved. Fix the comment that argues
otherwise. The same rule for Nudge and Rearrange, where a locked card inside a
moving fence is "held" (`ui/board-actions.ts`, the `held` set), needs a
decision when this is built. Keep them consistent: a fence that moves carries
everything in it.

**6. Delete says "Deleted N" with Undo.**
Cut does this (`state.ts:481`), the menu's Delete (`commands.ts:457-460`) does
not. Same toast, same `undoAction()`.

**7. Deleting one timeline step gets an Undo toast.**
`ui/timeline-view.ts:675-681` deletes at once, while the two larger deletes ask
first. Follow Remove stop in the tour editor (`ui/tour-edit.ts:506`): delete,
then a toast with Undo.

## Batch 2: Nudge and fences

**8. Nudge these N, and Nudge fence.**
Give `nudgeBoard()` a scope: the ids, the fence, or the whole board. It already
solves group by group, so a selection is the top-level group restricted to those
ids, with everything else fixed, and a fence is that fence's group and the groups
nested in it.
- Card menu with several cards selected: "Nudge these N", beside Rearrange these
  N.
- Fence menu: "Nudge fence".
- Board menu: right-clicking inside a fence offers Nudge fence instead of Nudge
  board, the same switch Rearrange makes (`ui/menu.ts:1045-1052`).
- The Arrange flyout (`ui/flyout.ts:165-188`): Nudge board, and Nudge selection
  under Rearrange selection.

**9. Fit fence to contents.**
A "Fit to contents" row on the fence menu, next to Rearrange fence and Roll up:
`fenceBox(null, itemBounds(members), baseStep())` plus the name plate, as the old
`enclose` code in `rearrange()` did (deleted in `450b9c9`; see git history for
the two-pass plate measure). Reset size on a fence means the same thing. Today
it filters fences out and does nothing silently (`ui/board-actions.ts:219-221`).
One undo step, with the members driven, since membership is measured by the
edges.

**10. Easier fencing.**
- Fence these N accepts one card (`commands/fences.ts:89-91`). The band path
  already fences anything.
- Cards too far apart to fence (`commands/fences.ts:64-68`, past `MAX_SIZE`):
  instead of refusing, offer "Gather and fence". That rearranges the selection
  about its middle (masonry), then fences it, in one undo step.

## Batch 6: Alt+drag

**23. Alt+drag duplicates.**
On a mouse, a drag that starts with Alt held leaves the originals where they
were and moves a copy. The copy includes what travels with the selection
(`travelling()`), so stuck notes and fence contents come too. Duplicate exists
as Ctrl+D (`canvas/input.ts:3373`, `commands.ts:491-494`). The question is
whether the copy is made at the press or once the drag crosses the slop. At the
slop, so a plain Alt-click makes nothing. The move and the copy are one undo
step. There is no `altKey` handling anywhere in `canvas/input.ts` yet.

**24. Alt+drag pulls a stuck note off its card.**
After it settles (`sticky.ts:121-122`, ten seconds), pressing a stuck note
drags its host (`canvas/input.ts:1730-1745`). The ways off are the menu's
Unstick or the arrow keys. With Alt held, the press grabs the note itself, and
the drop decides what it is stuck to as any drop does. Alt+drag on a stuck note
moves it rather than duplicating it: the note is what Alt reaches for there.
This needs a check that Alt+drag on a note never also duplicates.

**Alt on Windows** focuses the menu bar of the desktop app's title bar when it
is pressed and released alone. A drag holds it down, so it should not trigger
that, but check it in the Electron shell (`desktop/chrome/`).
