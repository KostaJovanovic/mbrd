# Open work, audited 2026-09-14

Every document under `research/` (top level, `docs/`, `old/`, `future/`) was read for unfinished items. Each item was then checked against `web/assets/js/`, `desktop/`, `mobile/`, `tests/` and `.github/`. Where a document and the code disagree, the code wins. `fixes-2026-09-14.md` and `mbrd-release-readme-draft.md` are done and are not re-listed, except G28's maintainer step.

## Decided by the maintainer, 2026-09-14

- The small bugs and the share-link gaps go on `research/rapid-fixes-2026-09-14.md` (R12, R13).
- The `.mbrd` format stays free to change until 1.0. v0.316 did not close the window, so moving the history out of `board.json` needs no compatibility step.
- The starting board gets no audio card.
- Dark mode and colourblind mode wait. No work now.
- No browser-driven test suite for the web app.
- No `meta.tilt`: exports keep leaning only the cards they can see.
- The undo size stays in the Timeline gear menu, and nowhere else.
- The research tidy goes on the fix list (R15).
- From the UI clutter items, the spacing rhythm and a phone-width pass go on the fix list (R17, R18). The two item-menu rows and the eleven-control note format bar stay as they are.
- A board keeps its assets inside the file, always. The "may assets live outside the file" question in `docs/mbrd-format.md` is closed.
- Fencing happens only on the canvas, on every platform. A drag in the Feed never changes which fence a card is in.
- In the Feed, the cards of one fence always stay together. Fences have no priority, so fence groups and loose cards interleave by position: loose cards, a fence, more loose cards, another fence (R14 checks the Feed does this).
