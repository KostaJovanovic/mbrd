# Refactor audit, 15 October 2026

Read-only audit of `web/assets/js`, in four slices, for refactor candidates:
duplication across modules, dead exports, oversized functions, missed reuse of
existing helpers, layering smells. Each slice writes its own section below as it
goes. Nothing here has been carried out yet.

## Merged ranking

59 findings, 55 once cross-slice duplicates are merged (icon(), reducedMotion
and motion timing, hex normalisation, mulberry32). Ordered by what to do first.

**Bugs found on the way** (behaviour, not refactor):

1. `exportAs` is a copy of `saveAs`, so "Export a copy…" rebinds and renames
   the open board (`commands/file.ts:310-312`, `storage/storage.ts:320-327`).
2. `boardArtefactName` strips with `[^\w -]`, so "Ćevapi" exports as
   `evapi.jpg` (`commands/file.ts:60-63`); fix through one `safeFileStem`.
3. `state.ts` `RECORDED_HASH_KEYS` is a third, untested copy of the meta-hash
   key list; a key added to `itemHashes()` and missed there leaves the timeline
   naming bytes Optimize let go.
4. Hand-rolled listener loops in `canvas/audio.ts`, `pen.ts`,
   `playlist-queue.ts`: one throwing subscriber skips the rest
   (`util.emitter()` isolates them).
5. Seven clipboard writes use `navigator.clipboard` bare, which is undefined
   on a LAN http origin; `copyText()` in `perf/view-perf.ts` already handles it.

**Tier A, high or medium payoff, low risk:** the meta-hash key lists;
the orphaned debugPerf/debugSound commands and `cueLogOn`; `icon()` and an
`svgEl()` down to `util.ts`; `copyText` to `util.ts`; `withLift()` in
`consent.ts`; `serial()`/`pool()` in `util.ts`; `zip.ts` record/EOCD/inflate
helpers; `canvas/links.ts` plus `bareHost()`; pointer-capture helpers to
`util.ts`; generic `mobileOrder()`; `Viewport.centreWorld()`; `editInline()`;
`openModal()`; `motionMs()`/`reducedMotion()`; ghost card into `ghosts.ts`;
tour mutations into `tour-model.ts`; Drive helpers in `commands/file.ts`;
`prepareFile()` stand-in table; `surfaceToPicture()`.

**Tier B, medium payoff, medium risk** (needs tests retargeted or a hand
check): `menu.ts` engine vs `context-menu.ts`; `mountEditor()` split;
`clean/music.ts`; `ui/hold.ts`; `canvas/note-composer.ts`.

**Tier C, low payoff, low risk:** everything else in the slices below, best
adopted as the files are touched.
