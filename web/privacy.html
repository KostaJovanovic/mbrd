Privacy

What mbrd does with your things, which is nothing at all - and this is the long way of saying so.

no account no upload no tracking last changed 14 August 2026

  1. Your things

    mbrd runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no sign-in and no server that holds your work: images, video, audio, notes and everything else you add stay on your own computer, in your browser's storage, and exporting a board writes a .mbrd file wherever you choose to put it. We collect nothing at all - no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no error reporting - and the app works with the internet switched off, so opening a board tells nobody. The other half of that promise is that clearing your browser's data for this site deletes your boards and we have no backup to restore them from, so export anything you would be sorry to lose.

  2. The network

    Three things reach the network. All three you have to ask for, and none of them sends your board. Pressing play on a YouTube or Spotify card loads that card from YouTube or Spotify, who then see the request the way they would if you opened it in a tab; the YouTube embed uses their no-cookie host. Generating a preview image for a video your browser cannot read downloads a converter from a public code mirror, jsDelivr - the converter comes to the video, never the other way round. And loading this page at all fetches it from our host, Cloudflare, which serves the site as static files and sees the traffic that reaches it the way every web server does; we have added no logging of our own on top.

  3. The rest

    There is no account to create and nothing is gathered, so a child's data is treated no differently from anybody else's - neither is collected. If any of this ever stops being true, this page changes first and the date at the top changes with it, and mbrd is open source, so you do not have to take our word for any of it. Questions go to the project's issues page.
