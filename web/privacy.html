Privacy

What mbrd does with your things. Last changed 14 August 2026.

mbrd runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no sign-in and no server that holds your work. Nothing you put on a board is sent anywhere.

Your boards and files

Images, video, audio, notes and everything else you add stay on your own computer, in your browser's storage. Exporting a board writes a .mbrd file to wherever you choose to put it. We never receive a copy, because there is nowhere for a copy to go.

Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes your boards. There is no backup on our side to restore them from, which is the other half of the same promise - so export anything you would be sorry to lose.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no error reporting. The app works with the internet switched off, and opening a board tells nobody.

The three times something leaves your machine

All three are things you have to ask for, and none of them sends your board:

  • Pressing play on a YouTube or Spotify card loads that card from YouTube or Spotify, who then see the request the way they would if you opened it in a tab. The YouTube embed uses their no-cookie host.
  • Generating a preview image for a video your browser cannot read downloads a converter from a public code mirror (jsDelivr). The video itself never leaves your machine - the converter comes to it.
  • Loading the page at all fetches it from our host, which like every web server sees the request. We do not keep it or look at it.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare as static files. They operate the network the page arrives over and see the traffic that reaches it; we have added no analytics and no logging of our own on top.

Children

There is no account to create and nothing is collected, so there is nothing here that treats a child's data differently from anybody else's - because neither is collected at all.

Changes

If this ever stops being true, this page changes first and the date at the top changes with it. mbrd is open source, so you do not have to take our word for any of the above - the code is there to read.
